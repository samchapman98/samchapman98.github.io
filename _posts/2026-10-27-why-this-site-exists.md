I want to get into the habit of writing in public, even when what I write is rough. Especially when it's rough.

The plan is simple: post things before they feel ready. Each piece gets a label for how finished it is, so nobody (including me) mistakes a sketch for a settled view.

If something here is wrong, that's fine. It's a sketch.
