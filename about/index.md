I'm Sam. I used to run Sentient Futures, a nonprofit working on the wellbeing of future sentient beings. Now I'm doing independent research on s-risks (risks of astronomical suffering) and cluelessness: whether we can ever tell which of our actions make the long-term future better.

Before that I worked in economics and data science, and advised the UK Government on policy.

This site is where I think out loud. Pieces are marked by how finished they are, and most are sketches. If you've found it, you're early. Hello.
